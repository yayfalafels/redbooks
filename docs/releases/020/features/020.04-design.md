_04.IS.02 (closed) a split on one date or across dates within its month_

**resolution.** Both, by path, DC.27. The data model keeps the split's own date and a date on each member, so it holds a loaded split whose records fall on different dates within one month. A split the user enters takes one date: the API accepts no member date, the ledger service writes every member on the split date, and the split form drops the payment source date field. AR.07 item 05, WF.09.02, PG.33 and the `split` notes of the **data model** state it. MR.14 and the seed cases keep the loaded dates.

**problem.** DC.23 sets the hard rule that every date of a split falls in one calendar month, and the `split_member` constraint of the **data model** enforces it. Within the month, each payment source keeps its own date, per AR.07 item 05 of the **accounting rules** and WF.09.02 of the **workflows**. The user's intent reads that a split is contained to a single date.

**why per-source dates stay for now.** The account `02` seed rebuilds a sample of its cost center groups as splits, MR.14 of the **data migration** and 02.SEED.25 of the **test cases**, and 4 of those groups fall on two dates one to three days apart, per DQ.14 of the **homebudget data review**.

**options.**

01. every date of a split equals the split date. A split builder group on two dates loads as separate transfers and expenses, as the account `01` history does, and the payment source date field leaves the split form.
02. per-source dates stay within the month, as the design reads now.

**docs to change once settled.** AR.07 item 05, WF.09.02, PG.33 item 03.04 of the **front end pages**, the `split` and `split_member` notes of the **data model**, MR.14 of the **data migration**, and 02.SEED.14 and 02.SEED.25 of the **test cases**.
