### 04.04 homebudget data review

_04.IS.01 (open) account 01 cost centers miss 0.00 at in-scope month ends_

**problem.** DC.23 makes CK.06 a failing check: every cost center holds 0.00 at every month end, since no split crosses a month boundary. The account `01` history breaks that rule inside the migration scope, 2017-01-01 to 2026-05-23. DQ.31 and summary item 09 of the **homebudget data review** count the misses after the 2017 reset:

01. the main personal cost center misses 0.00 at 2 of 113 month ends.
02. a household cost center misses at 7 of 32 month ends.
03. three other cost centers miss at 2 month ends each.

About 15 month ends miss in all, and every cost center is at 0.00 on the cutoff. DQ.31 loads the rows as-is, and MR.13 of the **data migration** compares the merged cost center with HomeBudget at every month end. With CK.06 failing, the statement build of each such month fails during the migration, per MR.13 and DF.14 of the **data flows**.

**not in conflict.** The account `02` history holds 4 splits whose records fall on two dates, 2026-05-24 and 05-27, 06-26 and 06-27, 07-12 and 07-15, and 09-12 and 09-13. Each pair sits inside one month, per answer 03 of the **homebudget data review**.

**options.**

01. the migration repairs each group that crosses a month end, such as by re-dating its records into one month, with a migration rule per group in the **homebudget data review** and a row per repair in the migration report.
02. CK.06 exempts migrated history, such as months before the account `02` start on 2026-05-24, and fails only on records written by RedBooks.

**docs to change once settled.** DQ.31 of the **homebudget data review**, MR.13 of the **data migration**, CK.06 of the **financial statements design**, and 02.MIG.08 of the **test cases**. The **data migration** also states the in-scope month-end misses, which it does not yet record.

### 04.05 data model

_04.IS.02 (open) a split on one date or across dates within its month_

**problem.** DC.23 sets the hard rule that every date of a split falls in one calendar month, and the `split_member` constraint of the **data model** enforces it. Within the month, each payment source keeps its own date, per AR.07 item 05 of the **accounting rules** and WF.09.02 of the **workflows**. The user's intent reads that a split is contained to a single date.

**why per-source dates stay for now.** The account `02` seed rebuilds a sample of its cost center groups as splits, MR.14 of the **data migration** and 02.SEED.25 of the **test cases**, and 4 of those groups fall on two dates one to three days apart, per DQ.14 of the **homebudget data review**.

**options.**

01. every date of a split equals the split date. A split builder group on two dates loads as separate transfers and expenses, as the account `01` history does, and the payment source date field leaves the split form.
02. per-source dates stay within the month, as the design reads now.

**docs to change once settled.** AR.07 item 05, WF.09.02, PG.33 item 03.04 of the **front end pages**, the `split` and `split_member` notes of the **data model**, MR.14 of the **data migration**, and 02.SEED.14 and 02.SEED.25 of the **test cases**.
